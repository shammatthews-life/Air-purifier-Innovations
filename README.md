# 🌬️ Air Pure Innovations: Air Quality Analytics & Dashboard

> **Transforming Environmental Data into Clean Air Solutions** — An interactive data visualization and engineering analysis project tracking air quality metrics, purification technologies, and design optimizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Indoor and outdoor air quality is a critical determinant of public health and modern living standards. The **Air Pure Innovations** project bridges the gap between environmental research and product engineering. By leveraging advanced data visualization and technical documentation, this project analyzes air purification methodologies, performance optimization strategies, and real-world pollution metrics to drive smarter, data-backed engineering improvements.

---

## 📊 Tableau Dashboard Preview & Link

Explore the fully interactive dashboard hosted on Tableau Public:

* **Tableau Public Viz:** [Air Pure Innovations - Air Quality Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/sham.matthew/viz/AirPureInnovations/AirQuality)

### Dashboard Highlights:

* **Pollution Tracking:** Visualizes particulate matter concentration ($PM_{2.5}$, $PM_{10}$), VOC levels, and environmental variables across different test environments.
* **Purification Efficiency:** Compares traditional filtration methods against modern innovative purification technologies.
* **Dynamic Filtering:** Allows stakeholders to filter data by filtration speed, energy consumption, and room dimensions.

## 💡 Key Insights & Engineering Concepts

* **Filtration Efficiency Curves:** Analysis reveals that multi-stage HEPA and activated carbon integrations reduce pollutant cleanup times by up to 45% compared to standard single-stage filters.
* **Energy vs. Performance Balance:** Optimized fan-speed algorithms can maintain optimal air exchange rates while lowering power consumption during off-peak pollution hours.
* **Design Improvements:** Data-driven recommendations for aerodynamic casing modifications to minimize airflow resistance and acoustic noise.
